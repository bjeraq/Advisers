## Premier entry criteria (2 sources, but thresholds reset yearly)
- **Average market cap ≥ KWD 78 million** (≈ median market cap of all listed companies) — boursakuwait.com.kw premier-market page ; Boursa 2023 segmentation PDF (docs.boursakuwait.com.kw/NewsPDF/0_NEWS_2023_E_812023124217555.pdf) ; aljarida.com/article/11255.
- **Average daily traded value (ADTV) ≥ a liquidity level Boursa sets each year** (e.g. **KWD 281k** in the 2023 review). Also must have been listed ~2 years (press).
- Main market: must meet Boursa's market-cap/liquidity levels and price ≥ par at year end (Oxford Business Group 2019 rulebook summary) — say only "Main has lower bars than Premier".

## What it means for a small investor (explainer, evergreen)
1. **Liquidity**: Premier stocks usually have many buyers/sellers → easier to enter/exit at a fair price, tighter spreads. Main stocks can be thinner; Auction stocks only trade in auction windows.
2. **Same circuit-breaker logic** (−5%/+10% per stock) applies to listed securities; index-level halts apply separately to Premier and Main indices.
3. **Promotion/demotion is a news event**: index funds/foreign investors tracking Premier may buy promoted names and sell demoted ones.
4. **Segment ≠ quality guarantee**: it is about size + liquidity, not profit or Sharia status.
