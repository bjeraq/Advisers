## Script-ready explainer (evergreen)
1. **AGM / board approval** → dividend + timetable announced on Boursa.
2. **Last day to buy (يوم الحيازة)** = 3 trading days before record date (T+3).
3. **Ex-date (أول يوم دون استحقاق)** = next trading day; buying from today = no dividend; price normally opens lower by about the dividend.
4. **Record date (يوم الاستحقاق)** = Maqasa checks who is on the register.
5. **Payment date (يوم التوزيع)** = cash to your account (often 3–5 days after record: NBK 3 days, Zain 5 days).
6. Kuwait weekends are Fri–Sat → count **trading** days, not calendar days (NBK's ex-date was a Thursday, record the following Monday).
- Fils reminder: 1 KWD = 1,000 fils; 35 fils on 1,000 shares = KWD 35.
