## Numbered steps (script skeleton)
1. **Get your investor number (رقم المستثمر)** from Kuwait Clearing Company (Maqasa): Civil ID, in person or Maqasa Online; your broker can guide you.
2. **Pick a CMA-licensed broker** (e.g. NBK Wasata, Al-Wasat, Commercial Brokerage, MEFBC, KFIC…) — check licence on cma.gov.kw.
3. **Open the trading account**: Civil ID + Kuwaiti bank IBAN/statement; many firms let you sign online via **هويتي**; sign the trading contract & KYC.
4. **Fund the account** (transfer from your bank to the broker/clearing account).
5. **Choose the stock & market** (Premier = more liquid). Check price, spread, news.
6. **Place the order**: prefer a **limit order** (you set the max price); market order fills at best available price. Day or good-till-date (≤90 days).
7. **Mind the clock**: pre-open 8:50–9:00, continuous 9:00–1:00, closing auction 1:00–1:10, trade-at-last 1:10–1:15 (Sun–Thu).
8. **Pay the fee**: about **0.15%** of the trade (min **KWD 0.5**) under the new CMA schedule — e.g. KWD 1,000 buy ≈ KWD 1.5.
9. **Settlement T+3**: shares/cash settle 3 trading days later; matters for dividends (see dossier 14).
10. **Track** on the broker app / Maqasa Online (holdings & registrar records).
