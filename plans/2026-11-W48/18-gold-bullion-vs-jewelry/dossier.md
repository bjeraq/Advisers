## Worked example (illustrative; 21k = 31.675, 24k = 36.200 per Youm7, 23 Nov)
Assumptions:
- Workmanship on jewelry: 2 KD/g (mid of the 1–3 range).
- Buy-back deduction: 0.2 KD/g (single source).
- Bar premium: 0.25 KD/g on buying, with a sale at spot minus 0.2 KD/g (assumption).

| | Buy | Sell same day | Loss |
|---|---|---|---|
| 10g 21k jewelry | 10 × (31.675 + 2) = **336.75 KD** | 10 × (31.675 − 0.2) = **314.75** | **≈ 22 KD (6.5%)** |
| Same piece at 3 KD/g workmanship | 346.75 | 314.75 | ≈ 32 KD (9.2%) |
| 10g 24k bar | 10 × (36.2 + 0.25) = **364.5 KD** | 10 × (36.2 − 0.2) = **360.0** | **≈ 4.5 KD (1.2%)** |

- Note: 10g of 21k contains 8.75g of pure gold. The bar holds 10g pure, so the two items are not the same amount of gold. Compare the loss %, not the price.
- Script line: "jewelry is for wearing. The workmanship is the price of the design and does not come back when you sell."

## Uncertainties
- Gram prices move every hour. Re-check on the recording day.
- Workmanship and buy-back deductions vary by shop and are negotiable. Present ranges, never a single number.
